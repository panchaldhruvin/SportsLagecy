# SportsLagecy
Sportslagecy
A web-based platform that connects students, athletes, and sports enthusiasts with the world of sports — exploring equipment versatility, discovering new games, and building a deeper understanding of how a single piece of gear can unlock multiple sporting experiences

SportsLegacy is a web-based information platform designed to collect, organize, and present comprehensive data about a wide variety of sports and the equipment associated with them. Built for accessibility across all devices — desktops, tablets, and mobile phones — no app installation is required.

The platform empowers users to explore the world of sports in a smarter, more connected way, bridging the gap between equipment knowledge and game discove
